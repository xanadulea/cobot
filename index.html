What I build

Built around how your team already works.

You don't need to change tools. I connect the ones you have and fill the gaps between them, with a person checking wherever a mistake would cost money.

  1. i.

    Admin that moves itself

    Orders, invoices, emails and spreadsheets that travel between your tools on their own, and flag anything that doesn't add up.

    For example: order sync, invoice checking, a weekly report that writes itself
  2. ii.

    Small tools made for you

    When off-the-shelf software is too big or doesn't fit: stock and storage tracking, order status pages, simple internal dashboards.

    For example: warehouse stock list, booking overview, a page your staff actually use
  3. iii.

    Follow-ups that don't slip

    Personal first emails and one polite follow-up, with replies drafted for your team to check and send.

    For example: partner outreach, quote follow-ups, booking confirmations
  4. iv.

    Research and content

    Posts, articles and short briefs on your market, researched with sources and only published after your yes.

    For example: social posts, a Monday competitor summary
Built for the bad days

Real systems break. Mine notice, recover and tell you.

Demos only show the good days. These are real problems from the system above, and what it does about them now.

  • What happenedA failed save made a job post the same thing every hour.
    What it does nowEvery action keeps a "done" record and checks for duplicates before it runs.
  • What happenedA scheduled job sometimes didn't start, so posts went out late.
    What it does nowA dispatcher starts each run, confirms it started and retries up to three times.
  • What happenedThe email provider blocked sending one morning.
    What it does nowIt saves the emails as drafts, sets a reminder and sends them on the next run.
