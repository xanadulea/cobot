Working demo · donation matching

Every donation matched to the right supporter, with a person checking the unclear ones

Charities often copy each gift from the bank statement and their online giving page into the supporter database by hand. This demo does the matching for a made-up animal rescue: clear cases are handled automatically, anything uncertain waits for a person, and the result is one file ready to import.

All names and data here are invented. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
